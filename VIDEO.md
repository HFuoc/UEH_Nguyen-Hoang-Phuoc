# Simulation demonstration

[Watch the simulation recording](SIMULATION_DEMO.mp4).

The recording shows actual camera images and wheel odometry captured in the Ubuntu ROS 2 environment. The clip is 299.87 seconds long, encoded as H.264 at 960x540 and 30 fps using simulation timestamps. The trajectory display is wheel odometry, not an official route-completion measurement.

The current driver does not finish the entire track. This simulation-only clip has no participant face or English narration; the assessed six-minute presentation required by the competition must be recorded separately.
